03 · Pair

Tide × Luna duo

Optional coexistence — if she wants both. Tide as day/flow; Luna as night/cycle. Shared oat ground, no competing chrome.

Duo concept

Small scene: Tide (simpler) and Luna (simpler) sharing a quiet shelf of paper light. Useful for onboarding “choose one — or keep both as companions.”

How they share space

Tide carries flow / day logging energy (teal). Luna carries cycle / evening calm (rose). Same face language so they feel like a set, not two products.

Product use

User picks a primary companion — or keeps both: Tide on Today empty states, Luna on evening / cycle moments. Duo mark for welcome or “companions” settings.

How to choose: pick Tide A/B/C, Luna A/B/C, or Tide + Luna as a pair. Expressions and phone moments above use the A · Simpler read for clarity — heroes show the full polish range.
