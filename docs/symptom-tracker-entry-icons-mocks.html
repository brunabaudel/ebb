Ebb · Entry icons · SF Symbols

Three log types, system marks

Apple SF Symbols for migraine, menstrual, and non-migraine entries. Browse the full library at sf-symbols.vercel.app. Companion: calendar mocks.

Design rules: No emoji. No brains or crosses. Migraine = rings/pulse · Menstrual = drop/dash · Non-migraine = square or line (never rings). SwiftUI: Image(systemName: "…") · tint with theme colours · iOS 17+.
