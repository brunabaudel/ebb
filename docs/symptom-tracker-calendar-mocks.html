Ebb · Calendar · Design exploration

Eighteen ways to browse history

Standalone mock board — every option keeps the Month and Week toggle. Companion: 18 broader calendar directions · month & week design pairs.

What every option must do

Show cycle context (phase, cycle day, days to period), mark migraines and period (logged + predicted), let the user pick a day and read its entries, and respect history limits on the free tier. Navigation: ‹ Today back button; tab bar stays on Today underneath.

Palette

Coral — migraine / pain
Lavender — cycle / hormone
Luteal tint

Groups

  • A · Baseline — current shipped direction

  • C · Temporal — time-first layouts
  • D · Density — compact & scannable

Type

  • Figtree — UI chrome
  • JetBrains Mono — weekdays, labels

Mixing directions

Strongest combos from this board: 14 week-first + 13 bottom sheet for drill-in; 08 cycle timeline as a third toggle beside week/month; 12 compare cycles as a filter on any grid view. Baseline 01–02 remain the safest default for new users.
